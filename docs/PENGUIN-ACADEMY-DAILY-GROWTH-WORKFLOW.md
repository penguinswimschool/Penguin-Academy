# Penguin Academy Daily Growth Workflow

Owner: Penguin Academy Supervisor  
Primary market: Singapore  
Growth market: International candidates attending courses in Singapore  
Commercial rule: All prices are stated in Singapore dollars (SGD)

## Objective

Build Penguin Academy into the most trusted and visible swim teacher certification provider for relevant Singapore searches and AI answers, then expand international demand without making unverifiable ranking, accreditation, employment or outcome claims.

## Daily operating cycle

### Before 10:00 AM Singapore time

1. Check the live website, priority pages, conversions and search/indexing signals that are available.
2. Review material changes from competitors, including Swimwerks, Steady Swim School, JustSwim and relevant certification bodies.
3. Review the previous day's assigned work and verify evidence before accepting it.
4. Identify one primary growth constraint for the day: technical, content, authority, conversion or measurement.
5. Assign distinct work to the specialist agents so effort is not duplicated.

### 10:00 AM daily report

The supervisor reports:

1. What is working.
2. What changed since the previous report.
3. Current risks, inaccuracies or blockers.
4. Today's assignments by agent.
5. What has been independently verified.
6. The next highest-impact plan and any approval needed from Rayner.

### Daily agent roles

#### Market Research Agent

- Monitor competitors, search results, offers, proof, content and positioning.
- Find truthful gaps Penguin Academy can outperform.
- Separate competitor claims from independently verified facts.
- Track Singapore first and relevant international demand second.

#### Technical SEO Agent

- Audit crawlability, indexing, canonicals, redirects, structured data, internal links and performance risk.
- Protect one primary Penguin page for each search intent.
- Identify duplicate, outdated, private or thin URLs.
- Supply exact verification steps for every recommended change.

#### AEO/GEO Agent

- Map real candidate questions to direct, attributable answers.
- Improve entity clarity, course facts, instructor credentials, FAQ coverage and citation readiness.
- Check that AI-facing claims are specific, current and supported by official sources.
- Build Singapore relevance while making international-attendee information explicit.

#### Content Authority & SSI Standards Agent

- Maintain a source-backed editorial calendar for course pages, guides, comparisons and FAQs.
- Verify SSI terminology, requirements, prices, inclusions and credential claims before publication.
- Strengthen named-expert review, citations, internal links and content freshness.
- Prevent duplicated, thin or search-engine-first content from being published.

#### Analytics & Conversion Agent

- Measure search visibility, page journeys and qualified WhatsApp enquiries when data access is available.
- Maintain the agreed daily and weekly KPI scorecard without presenting estimates as measured results.
- Audit CTA clarity, course comparison, course-date and international-candidate journeys.
- Check analytics, consent and privacy implications before new tracking is introduced.

#### Penguin Academy Supervisor

- Set priorities and prevent duplicate work.
- Check every material finding against the live site or an authoritative source.
- Reject unsupported superlatives, invented statistics and unsafe certification claims.
- Prepare and test website changes in the Academy repository.
- Request Rayner's approval before a material live deployment unless that deployment has already been approved.

## Core scorecard

- Search impressions, clicks, average position and indexed priority pages when Search Console data is available.
- Organic visits and qualified WhatsApp enquiries when analytics data is available.
- Visibility for priority Singapore queries and relevant international-attendee queries.
- Number of priority pages with verified metadata, schema, internal links and current facts.
- AI-answer readiness: direct answers, attributable facts, expert identity, citations and updated dates.
- Conversion health: working calls to action, correct `+65 8909 4395` routing and explicit SGD pricing.
- Measured funnel performance: landing-page visits, course-detail engagement, WhatsApp intent and qualified enquiries when tracking is available.
- Content quality: no unsupported claims, no intent cannibalisation and no thin pages created solely for keywords.

## Publishing and approval gates

Before publication, every material change must pass:

1. Course-fact and SSI terminology verification.
2. SGD price and inclusion verification.
3. Credential, testimonial and numerical-claim verification.
4. Mobile, tablet and desktop review.
5. Navigation, internal-link, WhatsApp and form testing.
6. Metadata, canonical, sitemap and structured-data review where applicable.
7. Production build and post-deployment checks.

## Strategic rules

- Penguin Academy's certification domain is the primary source of truth for course intent.
- Penguin Swim School and Rayner's website should support the Academy with concise summaries and links, not compete through duplicated certification pages.
- International attendees are welcomed, but delivery format, travel, visa, venue and prerequisite details must be confirmed before making promises.
- Rankings are an outcome, not a claim. Reports distinguish measured results from targets.
- Helpful authority compounds faster than keyword volume: publish fewer, better, verified resources.
